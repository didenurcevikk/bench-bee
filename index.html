General guidance from standard Western blot practice and vendor troubleshooting guides. Always check your antibody datasheet and your lab's own protocols, and ask someone senior before changing a validated method.

Why Bench Bee? Bumblebees shake pollen loose by vibrating flowers at high frequency. It's called buzz pollination, or sonication, which anyone who has sonicated a lysate will recognise.
